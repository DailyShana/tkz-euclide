\section{Installation}

\tkzname{\tkznameofpack} is available on \tkzname{CTAN}\footnote{\tkzname{\tkznameofpack} is part of \NameDist{TeXLive}, where \tkzname{tlmgr} installs it, and of \NameDist{MiKTeX} under \NameSys{Windows}.}. If you want to test a beta version, put the files in a texmf folder that your system can find.
Check the following points:

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{5pt}
\item The \tkzname{\tkznameofpack} folder must be in a path searched by \tkzname{latex}.
\item \tkzname{\tkznameofpack} uses the package \tkzNamePack{xfp}.
\item \PGF\ must be installed on your computer. \tkzname{\tkznameofpack} uses several libraries of \TIKZ:

 \begin{tabular}{l}
    angles,                         \\
    arrows,                         \\
    arrows.meta,                    \\
    backgrounds,                    \\
    calc,                           \\
    decorations,                    \\
    decorations.markings,           \\
    decorations.pathreplacing,      \\
    decorations.shapes,             \\
    decorations.text,               \\
    decorations.pathmorphing,       \\
    intersections,                  \\
    math,                           \\
    plotmarks,                      \\
    positioning,                    \\
    quotes,                         \\
    shapes.misc,                    \\
    through
\end{tabular}

\item This documentation was compiled with \tkzname{lualatex}, but \tkzname{pdflatex} and \tkzname{xelatex} can also be used.
\end{itemize}
